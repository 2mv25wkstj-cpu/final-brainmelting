Brain Melded

how excessive screen use can affect children negatively during their development

The National Library Of Medicine created a study about the excessive amount of screen time does to a Childs development. They say “Children's heavy reliance on screen media has raised serious public health issues since it might harm their cognitive, linguistic, and social-emotional growth.”. Throughout this entire article it shares how screens can help, but they also can hurt. A large amount of screen time reduces social interaction, stunts imagination, and has an impact on language development. Children cognitive development, language development, and social-emotional development gets affected negatively especially to a growing child as young as two or three years old going up to teenage years.

Screen Apocalypse

how too much screen time can cause emotional and behavioral problems, and the only answer to those problems is going right back to the screens

American Psychological Association had an Interview with Michael Noetel PHD when he mentioned that to much screen time is causing children to have emotional along with behavioral problems. He mentions that these problems that are created by the screens are only being solved with more screen time. Saying that the solution to the problem is the problem itself. Micheal Noetel had conducted his own study and says “The study revealed that the more children engaged with electronic screens, the more likely they were to develop socioemotional problems. This included both internalizing problems, such as anxiety and depression, and externalizing problems, such as aggression and hyperactivity. Conversely, children experiencing socioemotional problems were found to be more likely to turn to screens as a coping mechanism.” With this article shows how in order to save the kids we need to stop the screens from taking over. Before it gets to a point where they’re older and face hard emotions like anxiety and depression and with that they turn back to screens or something even worse.

Zombie Behaviors

49% of parents rely on children using their screens, and it's leading kids to have a shorter attention span

In current times children are using screens for everything and getting it from everyone. From schools to parents for learning and entertainment. How many time have you seen someone give their kid a screen to stay quite for a dinner in public? It’s very normalized to give a child something so they don’t have to think for themselves or face their own emotions. This is what the Children’s Hospital of Chicago wrote “Some parents also use screens to help manage behavior as 28% give into screen time to avoid a meltdown or tantrum multiple times a week. Nearly 3 in 4 (71%) have also used screens to help manage their child’s behavior in public, and 29% report to doing this most of the time.” Showing that parents are becoming dependent on screens to help raise their children which is doing nothing but hurt them in the long run.

The Cure

what's appropriate and inappropriate times to be on screens

The American College of Pediatrics says “The American College of Pediatricians (ACPeds) encourages parents to become media literate and limit screen time for their children. “ In order to keep our children off these screens we need to lead by example with limiting ourselves from screens. In order for them to put the screens down we have to keep a time limit on them and show them how we don’t need screens as a whole. Putting rules on the media can help them and yourself as a parent to not being so dependent on screens in any shape way or form. Doing things like reading books or coloring can help keep your kids stimulated and relax without watching things on a tablet.

Save the Brain

the pros and cons of screen time, with the page covering some positives that can come from screen time

This website isn’t to bash all screens or parents that let their children use screens its just to shed light that its not all good but its also not all bad. Solutions Health shows that there are pros and cons to screen time and here’s some pros “research suggests that electronic books and learning-to-read applications may improve young children’s early reading skills and creative thinking capacities”. Beginning at approximately two years of age, high-quality educational television programs can serve as an additional means for early language and literacy development in children.”. Without the rise of screens we wouldn’t have artist like Ms. Rachel and Gracie’s Corner that spread love and education to the children. So again yes there are some positive but that comes with screens being limited and used for educational purposes depending on ages.
