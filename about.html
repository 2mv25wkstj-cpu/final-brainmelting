About Us

What are our Personal Goals??

I want to spread awareness of the problem of giving children tablets and iPads at such a young age, because the laziness of current generations is dooming the upcoming generation instead of passing down the old lessons and teachings they grew up on.

I have seen a difference in children's behavior at home compared to at the center, and the reason is that parents let them have tablets or phones at home to do whatever they want.

What’s our Subject Matter??

Topic: In a previous assignment I made flyers for a fake museum exhibit about brainwashing by screens, called Brain Melting, rooted in the topic of nostalgic learning. I chose it to spread awareness about kids having too much screen time, and I want my subject to be kids' screen time while keeping the name Brain Melting.

Media: I want to use a lot of imagery along with real research and number graphs. The imagery will support the overall theme, which I want to feel more like horror or unsettling. But I don't want it to just be scary. I want it to also be understanding, which is why I need facts to back it up.

What’s our Form Factor??

Viewport: I'm designing for desktop because I want it on a big screen. A lot of older generations know how to use computers, and used them for things like work before using phones, so they can truly understand the website.

Input mode: I want the primary input to be the mouse (Fine input) because it's easy. I want people to have a lot of information to go through, and the last thing I want is for them to be overstimulated by all that information and by figuring out how to navigate the website.

Location / context of use: I want people to experience it at home so they can try to make a change right then and there, as soon as possible, in their own lives and their kids' lives.
