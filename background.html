Project Prompt

PT.2 Design and Deploy

Create a website that demonstrates your mastery of the technology and concepts behind designing for the web, as well as create an inventive site where you develop original content. You have the freedom, within some simple parameters, to develop your site's content and form as you like. The content guidelines are intended to provide parameters within which you explore meaning, content, concept and creativity. Your grade will include an evaluation of how compelling your response is within the broad content guidelines. Push your mind.

You must choose one of the following options for the content of your final project:

  1. Augment and develop content from another course you are currently taking, or took in a recent semester.

    I recommend this option for Design & Visual Communication students.

    • If you have taken/are taking DVCI, the Brand Book project is an excellent option for this website.
    • If you have taken/are taking DVCIII, you can extend any assignment from that class.
    • If you have taken/are taking Type II or Experimental Type, you can extend any assignment from that class.
    • If you are taking/have taken either Intro Type or Intro Design, extend a project you created in this class.
    • Students who are not in the DVC program should consider work they are doing in studio or academic courses, past or present, and how to best use that as the basis of a website.

  2. Create a website based upon substantial research.

    The topic must be thoughtful and must require a significant amount of deep research. You may conduct that research yourself if it is relevant to your topic. I recommend this option for BA students who are using Web Design to create their BA Project.

  3. Create a piece of net-art.

    I recommend this option for cTech or studio art students, particularly if your interests are "creative" rather than "professional."

Stay away from client work: Clients often fail to give you the site content/assets/feedback you need in a timely fashion that aligns perfectly with our semester schedule and timeline. Also, a client website should probably be done in a content management system, such as WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, or Etsy and have a database. However, if you would like to do a "client" website as a significant re-design of an existing site, you may propose that. Just don't work with or depend on the client to deliver content and feedback.

Stay away from portfolio sites: they also should be done in a CMS (such as WordPress), and portfolios have such a pre-determined outcome that they do not meet the project brief's objectives.
